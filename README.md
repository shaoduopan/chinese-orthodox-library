# Chinese Orthodox Digital Library
中文东正教数字文献馆

A personal digital library dedicated to preserving, digitizing, and studying historical Chinese Orthodox Christian texts, particularly biblical and liturgical publications associated with the Beijing Orthodox Mission.

## Project Goals

- Preserve historical Chinese Orthodox texts and their bibliographic information.
- Convert scanned publications into searchable digital texts.
- Provide online reading alongside original scanned editions.
- Support human proofreading and collaborative textual research.
- Make historical Chinese Orthodox literature more accessible to readers and researchers.

## Initial Collection

**Chinese Orthodox Psalter (聖詠經)**

- 1879 edition
- 1910 edition

## Editorial Principles

Digital texts may initially contain unverified OCR errors. Human proofreading will be conducted progressively, with corrections and revision histories preserved.

## Project Status

Early development. The initial online reading platform is under construction.
