中文東正教歷史文獻

Historical Chinese Orthodox Texts

收集、保存、轉錄與研究北京傳教團及其他歷史時期 的中文東正教文獻，為閱讀、研究與合作校訂 提供開放的數字平台。

A digital library dedicated to preserving, transcribing, and studying historical Chinese Orthodox Christian texts, particularly those associated with the Beijing Orthodox Mission.
